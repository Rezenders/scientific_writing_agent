# Reviewer Contracts Template

Use for author-approved interpretations of reviewer comments and response plans.

| ID | Reviewer comment | Author interpretation | Manuscript action | Response stance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R1.1` | `<VERBATIM_OR_SUMMARY>` | `<WHAT_THE_COMMENT_REQUIRES>` | `<EDIT_PLAN_OR_NONE>` | agree/clarify/disagree/partial | open/drafted/approved |

## Contract Fields

For each material comment, record:

- what the reviewer correctly identifies;
- what the manuscript can support;
- what must not be conceded, claimed, or implied;
- target passages and validation checks;
- whether implementation or evaluation evidence must be verified.
